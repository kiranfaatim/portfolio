Research Work

Synthesis and Electrochemical Characterization of Copper and Cobalt Phthalocyanine Composites Anchored on Reduced Graphene Oxide Nanosheets

Conducted comprehensive research on synthesizing hybrid composites of copper and cobalt phthalocyanines supported on reduced graphene oxide (rGO). Prepared and modified rGO nanosheets before anchoring metal phthalocyanine complexes to form conductive and catalytically active materials, then investigated them using spectroscopic and electrochemical techniques.
